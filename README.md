Idea is to create an application that will allow people to exercise without or going to gym. This application will mostly help to the people who have some issues with their back because of the sitting. They will have an option to modify the trainings based on their feeling and health and progress because there will be some days when they will not practice. Application will use standard exercise but it will be flexibile to for modifying based on the users needs
They will have a profiles, information about their health, how often they can practice, what they want to practice and where
